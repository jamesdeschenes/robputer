# Welcome to the Microdong Systems Robputer Project

At **Microdong Systems**, we’re on a mission to reshape the landscape of artificial intelligence. Our groundbreaking innovation, **Robputer**, is not just another AI—it’s a return to the roots of AI with a twist that sets it miles ahead of the competition.  

With its humble origin as a hacked-together Eliza program, Robputer has since evolved into something much more... *complicated*. It's an advanced AI system (sort of) designed to provide terminal-based interaction with a *special* personality. Whether it's helping, judging, or just idling in existential dread, Robputer is here to add some charm to your existence.  

---

## 🚀 Introducing Robputer: The Power of Eliza Reimagined  

In the race for more powerful AI, something vital was left behind. Robputer harnesses the incredible efficiency of a forgotten technique known as **Eliza**.  

Originally developed in the early days of AI, we’ve enhanced and scaled this method to achieve over **10,000x more power efficiency per inference** compared to the latest methods from so-called “leading” AI technology companies like OpenAI, DeepSeek, and Google.  

Robputer isn’t just a step forward—it’s a leap back to the fundamentals, reimagined for the modern era.

### 🌟 Features  

- 🐱 **Idle Chit-Chat**: Robputer keeps the silence at bay with sarcastic, insightful, or just plain weird commentary.  
- 😴 **Prompt Responses**: Ask it questions or give it commands, and watch Robputer respond with (sometimes) useful answers.  
- 🍁 **Made-in-Canada**: Finally, an AI that understands donairs, beaver tails, and Hawaiian pizza.  
- ⚡ **Unmatched Efficiency**: Robputer uses a fraction of the energy of other AIs, making it not only powerful but also sustainable.  
- 🧠 **Seamless Integration**: Thanks to our innovative *Human API*, Robputer interfaces with the world in a way that anyone can use—no technical expertise required. By utilizing familiar interfaces, humans become the API, enabling Robputer to be universally adaptable to any system, anywhere.

   ```bash
   python -m http.server
   ```
4. Open [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in your browser.
